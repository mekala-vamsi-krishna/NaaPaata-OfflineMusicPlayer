## How to Add Songs?

This app works with audio files stored locally on your device.

### Step 1: Add Audio Files
- Download or transfer audio files to your iPhone.
- Open the **Files** app.
- Navigate to:  
  **On My iPhone → Naa Paata Folder → Music**
- Move or paste your `.mp3` files into this folder.

### Step 2: Open the App
- Launch the app.
- The app automatically scans the **Music** folder.
- All detected audio files will be listed under the Songs section.

> 🔐 **Important Note**
>
> iOS does not allow apps to freely access files from arbitrary locations in the Files app.
> 
> For security and privacy reasons, Apple enforces strict sandboxing rules. An app can only access:
> - Its own container directory
> - Explicitly shared folders
> - Files provided via user-selected document pickers
>
> Because of this restriction, the app scans only the designated folder mentioned above.

Enjoy your music 🎧
