## 🎵 How to Add Songs

This app works with audio files stored locally on your device.

### Step 1 — Add Audio Files
1. Download or transfer your audio files to your iPhone.
2. Open the **Files** app.
3. Navigate to: **On My iPhone → Naa Paata → Music**
4. Move or paste your `.mp3` files into this folder.

### Step 2 — Open the App
- Launch the app.
- It automatically scans the **Music** folder.
- All detected audio files appear under the **Songs** section.

> 🔐 **Important Note**
>
> iOS sandboxes apps — an app can only access its own container, explicitly shared folders, or files from a user‑selected document picker. That's why Naa Paata scans only the designated `Music` folder above.

Enjoy your music 🎧
